Reading the plan. Each future week the buying plan uses max(committed, forecast) — never the sum. A committed order consumes the forecast (it's the forecast coming true), so it only raises the plan when it's larger than the run-rate. Whole Foods W35 (900) pushes that week up; Albert's W34 (250) sits under the forecast, so the plan stays at the run-rate.
Direct-ship nets against its container, not the warehouse. Pre-sold cases that go port → customer never enter your warehouse: they're excluded from stock, excluded from the shrink model, and they don't add to what you need to buy — the arriving container already covers them.
